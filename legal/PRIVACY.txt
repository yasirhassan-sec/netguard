NETGUARD PRIVACY POLICY
DRAFT - HAVE A QUALIFIED LAWYER REVIEW THIS BEFORE PUBLISHING.

What NetGuard collects
- Scan results (device IP and MAC addresses, hostnames, open ports, service banners, Wi-Fi
  network names and signal strength, findings) are stored ONLY on your computer in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\NetGuard. We never receive them.
- A local audit log of actions (scans started, reports generated, settings changed).
- Settings you enter, including SMTP and Telegram credentials for alerts. Passwords and tokens
  are encrypted with Windows DPAPI and can only be read by your Windows account.

When NetGuard contacts the internet
- Vendor database download (on request): fetches the public IEEE OUI list. No scan data is sent.
- Optional CVE lookup (off by default): sends detected software product/version strings
  (e.g. "OpenSSH 8.2") to the NIST National Vulnerability Database API. No IP addresses,
  MAC addresses or network names are sent.
- Update check (can be turned off in Settings): requests a small version file from
  [UPDATE URL]. Only your IP address and the NetGuard version (in the request) reach that server.
- Email and Telegram alerts (Pro, off by default): when you enable them, alert summaries
  (finding titles, device IP addresses, site names) are sent through YOUR mail server or YOUR
  Telegram bot to the recipients you choose. They do not pass through our servers.
- Licence activation is offline: keys are verified on your computer and nothing is sent.

Telemetry
- NetGuard contains no analytics or telemetry.

Deleting your data
- Uninstall NetGuard and delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\NetGuard.
