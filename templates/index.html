Before your first scan

    
NetGuard sends network probes (ping, port connections and service requests) to the devices you scan.
      Scanning networks without permission can be illegal.

    
    
      
  • Only scan networks and devices you own or have written permission to test.

  • Results are stored only on this computer.
    • 
      
  • The security score is guidance, not a guarantee. NetGuard can miss issues.
